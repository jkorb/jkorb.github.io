I am a mathematical philosopher at Utrecht University, working at the intersection of logic, metaphysics, and epistemology.

I am a member of the [logic group](https://logic-utrecht.nl/) at Utrecht University and an associate editor at [Philosophical Logic](https://www.philosophical-logic.org/).

I mainly lecture in Artificial Intelligence and Philosophy. I wrote an undergraduate logic textbook for AI, which is freely accessible under [https://logicalmethods.ai](https://logicalmethods.ai/).
