Digital Proof Tools for Philosophical Logic is a twelve-month project testing whether digital proof methods developed in mathematics can support substantive research in philosophical logic.

The technical case study is the open problem of characterizing bilateral equivalence (i.e. sameness of truthmakers and falsemakers in all models) in truthmaker semantics. The project will formalize the relevant semantic framework in Lean, use a human-authored and dependency-aware proof blueprint to guide proof search, and evaluate coding agents by checking their output against both Lean's kernel and the intended mathematical strategy. The aim is a reproducible workflow connecting philosophical arguments, formal statements, proof search, and verified proof objects.
